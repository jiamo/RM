# M0-SYMMETRY evidence

Task: prove the classical symmetries of the zero set of `ζ` inside the critical
strip, and use them to add further equivalent formulations of the Riemann
hypothesis.

Source identity: `RiemannHypothesis/Symmetry.lean` (new file, imported by
`RiemannHypothesis.lean`).

What is proved (all without `sorry`):

- `natCast_cpow_neg_conj`, `two_cpow_conj`, `pairedTerm_conj` — conjugation
  commutes with the individual terms of the eta series and with `w ↦ 2 ^ w`.
- `dirichletEta_conj` : `η(conj s) = conj (η s)` for `0 < re s`, directly from
  the paired series (which converges absolutely there).
- `riemannZeta_conj_of_mem_strip` : `ζ(conj s) = conj (ζ s)` for `0 < re s < 1`,
  obtained from `dirichletEta_conj` through the identity
  `η(s) = (1 - 2^(1-s)) ζ(s)`, the factor being nonzero in the strip.
- `riemannZeta_one_sub_eq_zero_of_mem_strip` : a zero of `ζ` in the strip
  reflects to a zero at `1 - s` (functional equation, `riemannZeta_one_sub`).
- `dirichletEta_ofReal_ne_zero` : `η(x) > 0` for real `x > 0`, because every
  paired term `(2j+1)^(-x) - (2j+2)^(-x)` is a positive real; hence
  `riemannZeta_ofReal_ne_zero_of_lt_one` : `ζ` has no zero on the real interval
  `(0, 1)`.

New formulations and equivalences (all `↔ ZetaStripForm`, hence `↔ ZetaForm`
and `↔ EtaForm` through `RiemannHypothesis.Main`):

| formulation | theorem |
|---|---|
| `ZetaUpperStripForm` (only zeroes with `im s > 0`) | `zeta_strip_iff_upper_strip` |
| `ZetaRightStripForm` (`ζ ≠ 0` on `1/2 < re s < 1`) | `zeta_strip_iff_right_strip` |
| `ZetaNonvanishingRightForm` (`ζ ≠ 0` on `re s > 1/2`) | `zeta_strip_iff_nonvanishing_right` |
| `ZetaSelfReflectiveForm` (each zero satisfies `s = 1 - conj s`) | `zeta_strip_iff_self_reflective` |
| `∀ H, VerifiedUpToHeight H` (verification at every height) | `zeta_strip_iff_forall_verifiedUpToHeight` |

Supported claim: the zero set of `ζ` in the critical strip is symmetric under
`s ↦ conj s` and `s ↦ 1 - s`, contains no real point, and the Riemann hypothesis
is equivalent to each of the five formulations above.

Not proven: the Riemann hypothesis itself, or any of the partial results of
`RiemannHypothesis/Partial.lean`.  In particular
`zeta_strip_iff_forall_verifiedUpToHeight` is an equivalence with the family of
verification statements over *all* heights; no single height is verified here.
