## The argument

Write `ω(x) = ∑_{n≥1} e^{−πn²x}` and `W(u) = e^u ω(e^{4u})`.

*The `Ξ` side.* Mathlib defines `Λ₀(s) = ½ · mellin f (s/2)` where `f` is the modified
kernel of the abstract functional-equation pair `hurwitzEvenFEPair 0`; on `(1, ∞)` one has
`f(x) = 2ω(x)`, and the pair is self-dual with `ε = 1`, `k = ½`, so `f(1/x) = √x f(x)`.
Splitting `(0, ∞) = (0, 1] ⊔ (1, ∞)` and applying `mellin_comp_inv` to the indicator of
`(0, 1]` sends the lower half to `∫₁^∞ x^{−w−1/2} f(x) dx`, giving
`Λ₀(s) = ∫₁^∞ (x^{s/2−1} + x^{−s/2−1/2}) ω(x) dx`. With `x = e^{4u}` and `s = ½ + iz`,
`dx = 4e^{4u} du` and the bracket becomes `4e^{u}(e^{2iuz} + e^{−2iuz})`, i.e. the integral
is `8 ∫₀^∞ W(u) cos(2uz) du`. Since `s(s−1) = −z² − ¼` and
`Ξ(z) = ½(s(s−1)Λ₀(s) + 1)`, this is `Ξ(z) = ½ − (4z²+1)∫₀^∞ W(u)cos(2uz) du`.

*The `H₀` side.* Termwise differentiation (justified by summable majorants uniform on
compact intervals) gives `W'' − W = 8Φ`, where `Φ` is the de Bruijn–Newman weight already
defined in `DeBruijnNewman.lean`. Hence
`H₀(z) = ∫₀^∞ Φ(u)cos(uz) du = ⅛∫₀^∞ (W''(u) + z²W(u))cos(uz) du − ((z²+1)/8)∫₀^∞ W(u)cos(uz) du`.
The first integral is exactly `∫₀^∞ P'(u) du` for
`P(u) = W'(u)cos(uz) + zW(u)sin(uz)`, and `P(u) → 0` as `u → ∞` by the double-exponential
decay of `W, W'`, so it equals `−P(0) = −W'(0) = ½`. The value `W'(0) = −½` comes from the
theta functional equation in the form `W(−u) = W(u) + sinh u`, differentiated at `0`.

Combining, `H₀(z) = 1/16 − ((z²+1)/8)∫₀^∞ W(u)cos(uz) du = Ξ(z/2)/8`.
